I lecture on AWS and Cloud Operations at **IDET**, and as an **AWS Community Builder** (Containers), I spend my time outside of work growing Sri Lanka's cloud-native ecosystem: speaking at universities, judging national hackathons, hosting a tech podcast, and running community meetups. I also founded **CryptX**, Sri Lanka's first island-wide Hackathon + CTF + Designathon.

When I'm not shipping infrastructure or teaching, I'm recording DevOps tutorials on YouTube, mentoring students, or exploring somewhere new with a camera in hand.
